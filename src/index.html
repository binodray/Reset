A few answers to help you find your rhythm.

How do I start, pause, or end a session?

Choose Start focusing to begin. The same button pauses and resumes your timer. Open the three-dot menu and choose End session to return to the clock.

Why does Reset show a clock?

Between sessions, Reset shows your local time. During a session, the flip display becomes your countdown, with a small clock in the header. Turn clock seconds on in Settings → Appearance.

How do I move or resize the widget?

Drag the header to move Reset. Hover over a corner and drag to resize, or choose a size in Settings → Appearance. Smaller cards hide extra details. On Mini, hover over the card to reveal the timer controls.

Where is Reset on the taskbar?

Reset lives in the Windows notification area, beside the clock. Check the hidden-icons arrow if you cannot see it. Left-click its icon to show the widget; right-click for Settings, timer actions, or Exit.

Can I keep Reset above other windows?

Open Settings → Window and choose Always on top. Choose Behind other apps if you want other windows to cover the widget.

Can I change the durations and reminders?

Set focus and break durations in Settings → Timer. Settings → Messages offers 10 break reminders, rotation, and your own message. Use Card text to customize the clock and focus messages on larger widgets. Choose Save preferences to apply your changes. A changed duration updates that countdown immediately; no reset is needed.

Does Reset need an account or internet?

No account is needed. The clock and timer work offline, and your preferences are saved locally on this computer.

How do I install or share Reset?

Run Reset-Setup.exe to install. Share that installer with others; it includes the Windows web runtime, so they do not need to install development tools or download a separate runtime.
